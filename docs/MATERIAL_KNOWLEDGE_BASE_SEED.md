# IUVFES Material Knowledge Base — Literature Seed

## Purpose

This document defines the initial literature/reference seed for the Material Knowledge Base. It is **not** a resonance fingerprint database.

All supplied entries are treated as:

- `source = LITERATURE`
- `classification = OBSERVED`
- `evidenceLevel = LITERATURE`
- `validationStatus = UNTESTED`

A reported operating frequency means that the cited extraction experiment used that frequency. It does **not** mean that the material or target compound has a validated resonance at that frequency.

## Knowledge boundary

Unless a source explicitly reports a measured value, the following remain `UNKNOWN`:

- `f0`
- bandwidth
- Q-factor
- resonance mechanism
- acoustic intensity when not measured/derivable from valid area and power data
- cavitation intensity
- fraction identity from frequency alone

## Existing-material evidence policy

Some newly supplied records refer to materials already present in the seed (notably Sirsak, Salam, Jahe and Kunyit). These are **additional evidence records**, not new material identities. The canonical material is resolved by normalized scientific identity + plant part; each source/protocol remains separately traceable.

## Initial priority

The supplied priority list is retained as a **data-entry / experimental-readiness priority**, not as a claim about scientific importance:

1. Nilam — relatively complete UAE parameters and quantitative outputs.
2. Bunga Telang — clear quantitative flavonoid and polyphenol outputs.
3. Rosella — quantitative yield and antioxidant outputs.
4. Salam — reported 40 kHz UAE condition and biological activity output.
5. Temulawak — UAE with RSM and power/time ranges.

## Intelligent priority factors

Experimental priority should later be calculated from evidence completeness, uncertainty, reproducibility, expected information gain, laboratory feasibility, and relevance to the active research question. A material with more literature is not automatically scientifically more important.

## Required next evidence

For materials selected for the IUVFES Material Trainer, the next useful evidence is a controlled frequency sweep with fixed or explicitly varied:

- material batch and initial mass;
- particle size and moisture where available;
- solvent and solvent ratio;
- temperature;
- pressure/vacuum;
- ultrasonic frequency;
- electrical power and measured acoustic power/intensity where available;
- amplitude and duty cycle;
- exposure time;
- analytical output and uncertainty;
- instrument, operator, dataset and provenance.

## Scientific rule

A literature frequency is never copied into `f0` automatically. A candidate `f0` can only be produced by response analysis of measured sweep data and must retain its evidence and model status. A validated profile requires independent experimental support according to the validation protocol.
